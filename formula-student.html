SHEET 01 PROJECT DETAIL — ROLES 2021 – PRESENT

Formula SAE

I joined Ravens Racing, my university's Formula SAE (Formula Student) team, in my first year and have been on it ever since. I started as a volunteer, went on to lead the aerodynamics subteam, then served as technical director while leading vehicle dynamics, and now lead vehicle performance and driver development. I've also driven the car every season. The team has taught me things a classroom doesn't: how to lead people, how to make design calls against rules and deadlines, and how a car looks from both the engineering side and the driver's seat.

Technical Director & Vehicle Dynamics Lead

Volunteer

What I took from it

  • Leading peopleRunning a subteam of volunteers means setting priorities and keeping people motivated, not just doing the engineering yourself.
  • Designing as a systemAs technical director I learned that the best part on its own isn't always the best part for the car.
  • Working to real deadlinesCompetition dates don't move, so scope, testing and manufacturing all have to be planned backwards from them.
  • Engineer and driverDriving every season taught me how to turn what a driver feels into something the team can measure and act on.
