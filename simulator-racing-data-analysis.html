The problem

Too often have I found that when sim racing or actually racing when I participated in Formula Student was data recorded and then nothing was done with it. In another case, someone would try to look at the data during testing or after the fact for working on a project and not understand what they are looking at or what it means.

What I built

Mainly having experience with MoTeC i2 through sim racing and Formula Student, I decided I wanted to improve the environment from the baseline to provide more meaningful information to drivers and engingeers to get a better understanding of what is happening to the car and inside it with the drivers inputs. This way during testing the data log can quickly be pulled and reviewed without need for additional software of complexity in the field. However, in addition to this I also wanted to be able to make easy to digest reports to summarize testing after it is completed. For this I used Matlab to sort relivant data for powertrain, vehicle dynamics, driver and aerodynamics and populate a Word document that can then easily be shared.
